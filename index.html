💡 How to use the Incident Report Tool:
  • Part 1 (Reporter): The person reporting the incident (Manager, Guest Relations, etc.) completes the incident facts, type of incident, employees involved, and recommendations.
  • Part 2 (Employee): The employee gives their own account of the incident in their own words, then signs (or the refusal is witnessed and noted).
  • Part 3 (Witness): Any witness records what they saw, independently of the initial report and employee's accounts. Use "+ Add Witness" if there is more than one.
  • Part 4 (HR): HR reviews all three accounts and closes the case.
  • View As: Use the dropdown below to show only the section relevant to whoever is filling it in — the other parts stay hidden (and out of that person's printout) so each account stays independent. Switch back to "Full Report (HR)" to see and print everything together.
